Effective: 31 May 2026 Updated: 31 May 2026

These Terms govern your use of the AltNet account / registration service at panmox.org (the “Service”) and the AltNet Studio application. By creating an account, registering a .alt name, or running a node, you agree to them.

The operator is Gabriel Wenzel, Prague, Czech Republic (“we”, “us”). Contact and abuse: abuse.report@panmox.org.

1. What AltNet is

AltNet is a peer-to-peer network for hosting websites and files under the .alt namespace. AltNet Studio is the open-source application that connects your computer to that network. There is no central server: content is stored and served by participating nodes (people’s computers). We operate an account / registration service and an always-on bootstrap node, but we do not host or control the content on the network.

  • Attempts to circumvent moderation, the content blocklist, revocations, or to attack the network or other users.

5. Your responsibilities

As a publisher: When you register a .alt name and publish under it, you are the publisher and are solely responsible for that content’s legality. We do not pre-screen the bytes of published content, but we review every name request before approval and act on reports.

As a node operator: Running a node means your computer stores and serves content-addressed pieces of other people’s content. The content is chunked and addressed by hash; you do not see and cannot choose what files you serve. AltNet Studio ships a blocklist of known-bad content hashes (CSAM and similar) that your node refuses to store or serve, and applies signed revocations that purge taken-down content. You can stop being a node at any time from the Node screen.

6. Moderation and notice-and-action

  • We review every .alt name request before it goes live.
  • Anyone can report a name via the in-app “Report a site” link or by emailing abuse.report@panmox.org. See Notice & Takedown for the formal notice procedure (DMCA and EU DSA).
  • We aim to review reports within 72 hours.
  • If a report is upheld, the name is revoked: a cryptographically signed deletion record is broadcast so the content’s chunks are purged from nodes across the network, and the name stops resolving.
  • We will provide the affected publisher a brief statement of reasons where required by the EU Digital Services Act, and you may appeal (see §8).
