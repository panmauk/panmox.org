AltNet Relay — Workspace Terms (Business mode)

Last updated: 11 October 2026

These terms apply to you if you create or own a workspace in AltNet Relay. They are in addition to the Terms of Service.

1. What being the owner means

When you create a workspace, you become its owner. You can:

  • invite people by @name;
  • remove members;
  • receive and decide on reports that members make about each other.

If you leave, ownership passes to the first member who joined.

2. Who you act for

  • If you create a workspace for an employer, company, school, club or other organisation, you confirm that you are allowed to do so. In that case, the organisation is responsible for the workspace and for how its people use Relay at work. "You" in these terms then also means the organisation.
  • If you create a workspace for yourself and friends, you are responsible for it personally.

3. Reports from members

Members can report a coworker to you. A report reaches only your computer, directly and encrypted. PANMOX never receives it. It can contain:

  • who made the report and who was reported;
  • the reason;
  • up to 10 recent messages the reported person sent to the reporter or in #general.

When you receive reports, you agree to:

  1. Use them only to deal with the problem reported. That means deciding whether to remove someone, and, if you act for an organisation, following its own procedures (for example HR or disciplinary rules). Don't use them to watch people or for anything else.
  2. Keep them confidential. Don't show the reporter's identity, the reason or the messages to anyone who doesn't need them. In particular, don't show them to the person reported, unless your organisation's procedure or the law requires it.
  3. Be fair. Where your role or your organisation's rules require it, give the reported person a chance to respond before you take serious action.
  4. Delete what you no longer need. Relay keeps reports only on your computer, at most 200 of them, and clears them when you remove the person or dismiss the report. If you copy a report anywhere else, you are responsible for that copy.
  5. Don't retaliate against someone because they made a report in good faith.
  6. Pass on illegal content. If a report shows something illegal that threatens someone's safety (for example threats of violence or child sexual abuse), contact the police. You can also report it to PANMOX at abuse.report@panmox.org.

4. Personal data and the law

  • For the personal data in reports, and in your workspace generally, you (or your organisation) decide how it is used. That makes you the controller under the GDPR for that use. If your organisation uses Relay for work, it must tell its people how their data is used at work, as it does for its other workplace tools.
  • PANMOX never has access to your workspace's messages or reports. We are not your processor, and we don't handle this data for you.
  • Inviting people to a workspace doesn't let you monitor them. Employers must follow employment and privacy law on monitoring employees (in the Czech Republic, for example, §316 of the Labour Code).

5. Things to know before using a workspace for work

  • Relay has no admin console, no message archive and no export, and no way to keep records for legal or regulatory purposes. If your organisation must keep business communications (for example in finance or healthcare), Relay is not suitable for that.
  • Messages are stored only on members' computers. When someone leaves or loses their computer, their copy goes with them.
  • Relay is a free service, provided as described in the Terms of Service. There is no service-level agreement.

6. If you break these terms

We can't see inside workspaces. If we receive a credible complaint that a workspace or its owner is used for abuse or illegal purposes, we may take action against the owner's account under the Terms of Service and the Community Guidelines. The owner can appeal.
