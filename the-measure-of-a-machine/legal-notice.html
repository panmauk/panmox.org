AI-generated content

What the characters say is generated as it happens by an AI language model running on the creator's own computer, and checked against their memories before it is said. If no generated line passes those checks, the program states the facts plainly instead, and a few short acknowledgements are fixed. There is no script. (The short line "All dialogue and voices are AI-generated" on the project page summarises this paragraph.) Their voices are synthetic, made with a text-to-speech engine's built-in stock voices. No recording of Brent Spiner, or of any other real person, was used to create or train these voices.

The characters' memories of the episodes were written for this project from the episode transcripts. When a character recalls a scene he was in, he can also recall lines spoken in it, taken from the transcripts. The language model was adjusted on dialogue from the episodes so that Data speaks in his own manner. Star Trek: The Next Generation and its dialogue are copyright Paramount / CBS Studios. Neither the transcripts nor the adjusted model are published here.

Because the dialogue is generated by AI, it can be wrong. It is not a statement by the creator, Paramount, Brent Spiner or anyone else, and should not be taken as fact.
