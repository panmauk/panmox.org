Privacy: The Measure of a Machine pages

This page covers only The Measure of a Machine on panmox.org: its project page, its technical documents (on the site's technical page), its legal notice and this page. The rest of panmox.org, including AltNet Relay and Studio, has its own privacy policy: https://panmox.org/legal/privacy.html.

These pages are run by Gabriel Wenzel, a private person in the Czech Republic (contact: panmox-group@panmox.org). The short answer: they handle almost no personal data.

What these pages don't do

No account, no contact form, no comments. No advertising, no tracking across sites, and no cookies set by these pages. (If Cloudflare's security systems ever need to check that a visitor is not a bot, Cloudflare may set a short-lived security cookie, __cf_bm; it is used only for that check, not for tracking.) The only script they load from another site is Cloudflare's visitor statistics (below); they load no fonts from other sites.

Hosting and server logs

The pages are hosted and delivered by Cloudflare, Inc. Like every web host, Cloudflare automatically processes technical details of each visit: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and your browser type. If a page fails to load, your browser may also send Cloudflare a short network-error report. Cloudflare does this to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks, and keeps these records under its own retention rules (https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). We do not use these records for anything else and do not combine them with other data. Cloudflare is a US company; transfers to it are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running working, secure pages (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Visitor statistics

These pages use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. A small script from static.cloudflareinsights.com sends Cloudflare the page address, the page you came from, your browser type, and how long the page took to load; like any request, this includes your IP address. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and does not track you across sites or fingerprint you. We see only totals (for example, how many visits a page had), never who visited. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how the pages are used (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). A content or script blocker in your browser stops it.

Newsletter

If you sign up for the PANMOX newsletter on one of these pages, the email address you enter is sent through Google Apps Script (Google) and handled as described in the main privacy policy: https://panmox.org/legal/privacy.html. Nothing is sent until you submit the form.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email only to answer you, and delete it when the matter is finished.

Your rights

You can ask what data about you we hold, and have it corrected or deleted, by writing to panmox-group@panmox.org. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (https://uoou.gov.cz).
