When someone knocks

You see who it is and what they wrote. Accept and you can talk. Decline and that’s the end of it.

Block or report, quietly

You can block or report anyone. They aren’t told. When three different people report the same account, it’s banned automatically. A ban can be appealed by email.

// Personal and Business

One account.
Two sides.

For friends, family and your people. Chat one to one, or make a group.

  • Groups of up to 32 people.
  • Everyone you invite decides whether to join.
  • Voice and video calls, one to one or in a group.

For a company or a team. Same account, kept separate from your personal chats.

  • Workspaces of up to 50 people, invited by name. Each person accepts or declines.
  • Coworkers can message and call each other directly.
  • Video meetings of up to 8 people.

Channels

Like #design or #launch: a place for one topic, for the people who need it.

You choose who’s in

Make a channel and pick its members. Nobody else sees it.

Reports stay at work

At work, a report about a coworker goes to the person who runs the workspace, not to us.

// Questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. The account is free, and there are no ads.

Do I need a phone number?

No. You sign up with an email and choose a name, like @rob. People find you by that name.

Can PANMOX read my messages?

No. Your messages are locked on your computer before they leave, and only the person you write to can open them. We can’t read them either.

Is there a phone app?

Not yet. AltNet Relay is for computers: Windows and Linux from Friday 16 October, and Mac later in October.

Why does Windows warn me?

The installer isn’t signed yet, so Windows doesn’t recognise it and shows a warning. The download section shows what to click.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account, then Delete account. It removes your account and everything on your computer. Your @name is never given to anyone else, so nobody can pose as you.
