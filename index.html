// WHAT IS ALTNET

A peer-to-peer internet.

The web was supposed to belong to everyone. Three companies took it. We’re building it back.

Drag a folder. It’s a website.

No hosting account and no monthly bill. Publish a folder from AltNet Studio and it’s on the network, at an address that ends in .alt.

Get AltNet Studio →

Your name is yours forever.

Claim a .alt name and it’s your address on the network. One free AltNet account signs you in to both Studio and Relay.

About AltNet →

Everyone who runs it helps carry it.

Run a node and your computer helps pass sites and messages along. There’s no single company in the middle holding it all. Try the network on the right.

Apps made to be simple.

// WHY WE BUILD

We don’t have to put up with being watched.

Constant surveillance is not something we have to tolerate, and neither is companies taking our data to train their AI models. Right now, most of us are being exploited by megacorporations that care about one thing: how much profit they make next quarter.

The alternatives do exist, but they’re often hard to use. Nobody wants to spend their time in a terminal, typing commands by hand.

So we build open-source software that is actually easy to use.
